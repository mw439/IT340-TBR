About TBR

TBR is an e-commerce bookstore that offers both physical and digital books. Our goal is to make reading more affordable, easier to explore, and enjoyable for all types of readers.

We use book reviews, community discussions, and a unique tagging system that includes themes, content warnings, and common tropes to help readers discover books that match their interests—without spoilers.

Our Mission

To create a warm, library-style digital space where readers can buy, read, and talk about books in one place. We want to make books easier to access, easier to understand, and easier to enjoy.
